Grammar Focus

The Future (Will)

The Sorting Hat is magical. It looks at a student and makes a PREDICTION about their future.

To predict the future, or to make a decision now, we use WILL + Verb.

  • "You will be a great wizard." (Prediction)
  • "Slytherin House will help you." (Prediction)
  • "I will put you in Gryffindor." (Decision)

Grammar Focus

The Future (Won't)

To say negative things about the future, we use WILL NOT or WON'T.

  • "I won't go to Slytherin!"
  • "You won't regret this choice."

🗣️ Prompt:

Name one thing you will do this weekend, and one thing you won't do.
